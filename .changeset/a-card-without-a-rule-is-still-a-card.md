A card of the offer poster has a surface even where the host names none.

The palette carried one value per quiz and nothing for a quiz a host has no
rule for, so a poster showing a package whose quiz ids the host's stylesheet
does not know stood there with TRANSPARENT cards: names and motifs floating on
the page, no colour anywhere, and nothing on screen saying that package and
host were out of step. That is exactly what happens the moment a corpus renames
its quizzes - the one case where the room should say least about the build and
said everything.

`--quiz-select-card-quiet` is that surface: the same grey paper three of the
quizzes carry. A host sets it as the default of its card and keeps naming only
the colours it wants to differ.
